# OpenSCAD Projects

## Brookville Shunter Locomotive

![Photo: 3D Render of the Brookville Shunter](./Vehicles/Brookville-Shunter/Brookville-Shunter.png)

A scaled-model replica of the original Brookville Shunter situated at the historic Te Aroha Railway museum in New Zealand.

- Multiple scale options: 1:4.5 (7.25 inch gauge mini rail), 1:8.4 (5 inch gauge), 1:50, 1:25, and full-size
- Detailed working components: cab + nose, chassis, flanged wheels, working journal assembly with axle bearings and suspension

![Render: Journal assembly with axle bearing and suspension](./Vehicles/Brookville-Shunter/Renders/Journal-Assembly/Brookville-Shunter-JournalAssembly.png)

## XTR-Street 2 LED Mount

Custom mount for rear-facing LED lights on Blade-Z XTR Street II e-scooter
[Read More >>](./Vehicles/BladeZ/XTR-Street2/README.md)

![Photo: LED Mounting Bracket for Blade-Z XTR Street II](./Vehicles/BladeZ/XTR-Street2/Renders/bladez-rear-side-lamp-mount-with-lamp.png)

This OpenSCAD design provides a mount that offsets the LED light from the chassis and straightens its alignment, fitting seamlessly with the scooter's wheel guard.

- Compatible with Blade-Z XTR Street II
- Reuses existing M6 holes from wheel guard

## Lathe Pulley Spacer

![Photo: Lathe Pulley Spacer](./Tools/Lathe/Pulley%20Spacer/Renders/lathe-pulley-spacer.png)

A precision-engineered spacer designed for lathes to maintain proper pulley alignment and enable belt tension adjustments on the spindle shaft.

- Configurable bore diameter for different spindle shaft sizes
- Includes half-cut versions for easy installation without dismantling the pulley

## Motor Brush Cap Removal Tool

Specialized tool for safely removing motor brush caps on brushed DC motors for maintenance access
[Read More >>](./Vehicles/BladeZ/XTR-Street2/README.md)

![Photo: Motor Brush Cap Removal Tool](./Vehicles/BladeZ/XTR-Street2/Renders/motor-brush-cap-remover.png)

3D printable tool designed to safely remove damaged motor brush caps from brushed DC motors, commonly found in electric lawnmowers and power tools.

- Features two pin holes for safe extraction without damaging the motor housing
- Specifically designed for Enviromower 24 volt Brushed DC motor 
- Can be modified for similar brushed DC motor applications
